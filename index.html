Learn AI Design with Phoebe

Two tracks into design where taste still decides

Generation got cheap, so judgement got expensive. A generator will hand you forty screens in a minute and not one opinion about which is right - and the average of everything on the internet is, by construction, not a point of view. A leader track gives design and brand heads the tool map, the IP and provenance reality, the craft-and-careers question, and a rollout that raises quality instead of just throughput. A designer track takes working designers through one real feature - the shared summary in Cadence, an AI meeting-notes app - from constraint brief to a spec'd, system-conformant, handed-off screen set. A live critique lab runs on the key pages: it renders a real screen, measures it for real (WCAG contrast ratios, computed spacing, hue families), and climbs 17 → 100 as you turn on the five craft levers - then loses 19 points the moment you reach for the sixth.

The designer track 🎨 · for product, brand & design-adjacent builders · 10 x 45 min · no coding needed
